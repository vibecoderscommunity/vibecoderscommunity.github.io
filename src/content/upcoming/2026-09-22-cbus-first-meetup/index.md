Vibe Coders Columbus #1 is kicking off something special, the very first Vibe
Coders meetup in Columbus and the first one in the United States. Inspired by
the energy of the Japan and Singapore Vibe Coders meetups, this gathering brings
that same creative spark to downtown Columbus for an evening of good
conversation, fresh ideas, and real people building interesting things.

Come hang out, meet other coders, swap stories, talk tools, side projects,
experiments, wins, bugs, and the weird little moments that make building
software fun. Whether you are deep in your craft or just curious about the Vibe
Coders world, this is a great chance to be there at the very beginning, meet the
people shaping the local chapter, and help set the tone for what comes next.
Hosted by Brandon Jewell and Rick Power at Improving Columbus.

The double doors past the elevators in the lobby is where you will find us!
