We're celebrating our tenth meetup by inviting folks to share anything they've vibe coded for fun. 😉 👏 It could be a game, a silly thing, or even a micro interaction that made you smile. 🌈

This is the space to share personal apps or games you made, and the **tools** / **workflows** you used! Please _do not bring_ apps intended to be commercial.

## Shareouts

Each shareout is ~5 minutes followed by a quick Q&A. Share

- Why you made it
- Favorite tool/plugins/extensions/skills used during the process
- Show the thing!!🥳

If you would like to volunteer to share a demo, please let us know through our form: https://forms.gle/QRHSTZX22qZM7wBP8
