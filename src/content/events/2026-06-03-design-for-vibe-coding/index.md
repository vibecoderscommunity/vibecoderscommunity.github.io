⚫️ Takeaways ⚫️ 

💡 Designing with AI in code requires starting with minimal AI-generated UI and iterating directly by describing problems rather than prescribing solutions.

⚡ AI-assisted creative tools should preserve your agency by offering geometry-based suggestions instead of fully generating the work for you.

➕ There is no single perfect tool for design vibing, so compare outputs across platforms to balance their unique styles and token costs.

🚀 Better prompting results come from clear specs, examples, and repeated iteration, and you can even use a stronger model to rewrite a rough prompt before sending it to a coding agent.

🧠 Develop transferable prompting and spec-writing skills while learning to recognize and avoid generic AI design patterns.
