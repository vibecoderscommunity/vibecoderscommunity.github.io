Vibe Coders SG #3 — Designing with AI, without the slop (Apple Platforms
Edition) 🔥 took over the Apple Developer Center Singapore, together with
iOS Dev Scout. The focus was on the workflows and techniques that help you
design with AI without the AI slop.

## Demos

- 📸 @soons presented **Pico cam**: a tiny instant camera for iPhone that turns
  the Dynamic Island into a hidden Polaroid.

- 🐾 @ReynardLee & May Yee presented **Dojojojo**: Tamagotchi-meets-Duolingo for
  fitness, where workouts feed a pixel pet, a card collection, and even a
  computer-vision push-up checker — built with design-reference guardrails so
  the AI output stays on-brand.

- 🚀 Meng shared the new **DesignCode 5** and the importance of incorporating
  taste in the age of agentic coding, through a live SwiftUI + Codex demo
  showing how agents can ship a full iOS app with a single prompt. He also
  shared how the craft left for humans has now shifted to taste — to create
  amazing macOS apps like DreamCut, and more!

- 🖱️ @jiachenyee presented **Liquid Glass on Apple Platforms**: why an app can
  be "great" and still "not feel right," and how Apple's new design system can
  help you fix it using native controls first.

- 🤖 @liyuxuan presented **LAIOS**: a local multi-agent orchestration system he
  built for himself, as well as some strategies to build great apps — roles vs.
  disposable agents, plan-before-code, self-improving loops, and a live graph of
  personal taste and AI-curated moodboard used to build Joodle, his amazing iOS
  doodle journaling app.

- 🍜 @Kimberley presented **Jiak Simi**: a Singapore palate compass that learns
  how you actually eat, then answers _jiak simi?_ with hawkers, halal, walking
  time, and a why — not what's trending or paid.

- ⏰ @edmundlim presented two personal tools: an **AI usage-limit tracker** with
  a home-screen widget (and a ping when your plan resets), plus a **sticky-note
  reminder board** synced across phone and Mac.
