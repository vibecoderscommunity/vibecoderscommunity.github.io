Every month someone asks which model they should be using, and the honest answer
is "it depends". So this time we're doing the whole spread in one night.

## What to expect

- **Shareouts** — short, informal demos from whoever wants the floor. Local
  models, hosted APIs, the setup you're quietly embarrassed by. All welcome.
- **Hands-on time** — bring a laptop. Compare what actually runs well on your
  hardware against what you're paying for in the cloud.
- **The usual** — snacks, questions, and people happy to help you get unstuck.

## Who it's for

Anyone curious about AI models, whether you've shipped something or you're still
on your first prompt. Come excited and engaged — that's the only requirement.

## Summary

Thanks to everyone who joined our 9th Vibe Coders Tokyo meetup! A huge thanks to Le Wagon Tokyo for hosting us and to all the speakers who shared their incredible experiences with models today. 🤩 ✨ Look out for our tenth event in September!

## Demos

Your projects were absolutely wild!

- 📱 [LLM on phone](https://jongaul.com/vibe-coding-slides/): Jon Gaul showed us how to scrape sites, run sub-agents, and manage files on a commute using Termux and Tailscale instead of standard AI apps.

- 🌐 [Weblingo](https://weblingo.app/): Francois demonstrated a tool that dynamically translates complex SPAs into multiple languages by understanding the full page intent.

- 🦖 3D destruction: [Ricardo Cabello](https://ricardocabello.com/) blew our minds by using Cesium 3D tiles to build a Katamari Damacy-style game and a burning Shinjuku simulator running entirely in the browser!

- 🤖 [Token logic](https://github.com/sunveda/vibe-coders-tokyo-9/blob/main/exports/How_Much_Should_Your_Coding_Agent_Think.pdf): Shin broke down the real cost of tokens and when to use light, medium, or deep reasoning depending on the complexity of your coding tasks.

- 🔬 [Local deep research](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research): Stefania Druga shared an awesome resource for running deep research locally.

- 🌊 [Nankai Trough](https://nankaitrough.bymarsel.me/): Marsel Bait shared an incredible mapping project visualizing the Nankai Trough.

Lastly, we scored 9/10 on the [guess-the-designer.replit.app](https://guess-the-designer.replit.app) game! Well done team 👊
