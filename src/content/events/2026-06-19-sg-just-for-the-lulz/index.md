Thank you to everyone who joined us to share the apps you built for yourselves. From a cozy garden space to an orbituary photo filter, we're happy to see you build things just for you!! 🤩 

A huge thanks to all our brave presenters, and a special shoutout to Hustle Singapore for hosting us! :nuu: 

## Here's what got demoed:

- 🌍 🌙  Xinni  @mushopea  presented an agentic OS built with Claude routines that uses multi-agent orchestration for daily news and travel planning.

- 🍁 Cassandra presented Seasons: A cozy journaling app featuring a blind-box reward system and tactile digital textures.

- 🎬  Melvin @carillus  presented a custom tool built for video creation pipelines that provides direct feedback for comic artists and voice actors.

- 🧘‍♀️ Alifa presented Ami: A Telegram-based lifestyle assistant that books yoga classes and extracts event details from Instagram screenshots.

- 💐 Celeste presented https://www.softgarden.studio/: An interactive space designed for calm and intentional experiences without productivity metrics.

- 📸 Thu Ya demonstrated a photo editing studio: A practical tool for transferring facial expressions, changing outfits, and upscaling low-resolution images.

- 🎵  Sasha shared a music app: An interactive tool that matches a user's current feeling to lyrics from song tracks.

## 🎞️ All 7 demos are now live on the [VC SG Mixtape Site](https://vcsg-mixtape.vercel.app/
