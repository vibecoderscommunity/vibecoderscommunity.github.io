### "_Not good enough._"

Don't worry — nothing about your childhood trauma here. This session was about
making sure our vibe coded apps are great quality, and what it takes to build
an app users will love using.

## What we did

- ⚡ **Lightning talks** on QA'ing vibe coded apps and automating tests so
  they're ready for launch 🚀, with Q&A. Catch up on
  [the talk slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bV4vVWDYKmB5ngRLavhjYtE6KlZhOzJA/view?usp=sharing).
- 💻 **Hands-on time** on our laptops, trying out the new skills, followed by
  group exercises and shareouts on how everyone keeps their app quality up.

Thank you to [Le Wagon](https://www.lewagon.com/tokyo) for hosting us in
Meguro, and to [MagicPod](https://magicpod.com/en/) for sharing their QA
automation expertise and keeping us fed with snacks! 🍙
