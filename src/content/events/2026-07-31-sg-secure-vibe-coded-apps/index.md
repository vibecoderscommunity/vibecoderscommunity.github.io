Vibe coded something cool… and then wondered whether it's actually safe to put it in front of real users?

This is a hands-on session with 2 lightning workshops, on how to take a vibe-coded app from "it works" to "it works without leaking credentials and my data everywhere."


## The line-up

We'll have one workshop/talk, with a lightning talk, plus Q&A for both!

1. By Ilia Tivin — Securing your vibe-coded apps and agents
    25-30 minutes
    • What changes when an agent/harness helps you build
    • The security mistakes worth caring about first
    • How to get your agent/harness to stop being so reckless
    • What to delegate to tools, platforms, or… actual humans.
    Ilia helps run VCSG, and is a consummate self-hoster.

2. By Aashish Vivekanand — How he scaled and secured his vibe coded application for 8000 users, across 4 platforms, on ~$20 a month.
