Vibe Coders SG #0 — Cosy Demo Nights ✨ was a huge success!!  🇸🇬

29 builders. 10 demos. Full of fun vibes as a community!

When we first started planning this, we genuinely didn't know if anyone would show up.. They did — and they demoed things that mattered to them.

Here's what got demoed live:

- 🤖 Nexus — @Elijah  demoed a fleet of open-source agents managing self-hosted infrastructure, orchestrated through Discord

- 🐾 Dojojojo — Reynard Lee & May Yee Lai built a gamified fitness app where your pet grows as you work out — track steps, nutrition, and exercise, then take your pet into auto battles with Vision AI tracking your moves in real time

- 🧓 Senior Says — Kay T & Pradnya Nir built voice-enabled forms for senior care volunteers to reduce manual record-keeping

- 📝 Joodle — Yu Xuan shipped an iOS app to draw one doodle a day and capture the moments that matter

- ⏳ Things Take Time — Chester built an iOS app for tracking the things that matter — longest demo of the night, earned every minute

- 🅿️ Parking by EigenMoto — @Eigen  coded a real-world parking solution for motorcyclists

- ✅ Can Check — @saiful anuar  built a community app to make social assistance matching simpler and more accessible

- 👁️ Aid for the Blind — Freddy built an accessibility app for the visually impaired

- 💼 Grunt Work → Impact — Cheryl demoed AI agents that automate service design workflows — not to replace designers, but to bridge the tech gap, de-risk decisions earlier, and give back time for the work that matters

- ✨ Handmade — Reza Ilmi built a design mode on top of your code, helping non-technical designers push that final 20% of polish

All 10 demos are now live on the [VC SG Mixtape Site 🎞️](https://vcsg-mixtape.vercel.app/)

Massive kudos to @carillus for stepping up to help with the transcribing and decoding of summary to make the new mixtape site a reality!!
